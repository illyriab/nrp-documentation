Topics that apply across repository systems and across phases. They are not services
the platform provides — they are decisions and obligations that come with running a
repository, whichever system it is built on.

## Decide these before you build

These become part of the stored data or of what depositors agree to. Changing them
later means migrating records or breaking citations.

- **Metadata profile** — records are stored in it.
- **Controlled vocabularies and authorities** — their values live inside records.
- **Persistent identifiers and how they are assigned** — they are cited.
- **Mapping of your metadata to CCMM** — harvesting into the NMD depends on it.
- **Licences you offer, and the deposition licence** — depositors agree to them at submission.
- **Personal or otherwise sensitive data** — affects the metadata model, access control and storage.
